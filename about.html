Our Story

Founded in 2025, Cave Cloud BD emerged from a simple idea: provide Bangladeshi businesses with enterprise-grade cpanel hosting at affordable prices. We saw a gap in the market where local businesses were forced to use international providers with poor local support and high latency.

Today, we're proud to serve over 100+ businesses across Bangladesh, from startups to established enterprises. Our infrastructure is optimized for Bangladeshi traffic patterns, ensuring blazing-fast speeds for local audiences.
